I currently work at Anyverse Dynamics in Shanghai.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), first at the School of EEE under the supervision of Prof. Xie Lihua, and subsequently at the School of MAE advised by Prof. Mir Feroskhan. I received my B.Eng., M.Eng., and Ph.D. degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT).

My current research focuses on Embodied AI, including Diffusion Policy and World Models with robotics. My doctoral research focused on SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping).
